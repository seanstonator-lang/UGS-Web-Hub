Publisher Standards

Advertising & Content Standards

UGS Web Hub separates written library content from fullscreen player screens. The goal is a cleaner visitor experience, clearer publisher content, and fewer pages that exist only as launch surfaces.

Where Content Lives

The hub, guide pages, collection pages, and generated library entries provide the readable content layer of the site. Each library entry gives context, playing notes, related picks, and a launch path before the visitor enters the actual player.

Where Ads Should Not Live

Fullscreen player screens are intentionally kept separate from advertising. They are behavior-focused pages where the visitor is there to play, not read. Keeping ads away from those screens reduces the chance of placing ads on low-context or navigation-only surfaces.

Quality Rules For Future Updates

  • New public pages should include useful written context, not only links or buttons.
  • Game player pages should stay lightweight and focused on the game experience.
  • Advertising should only be considered on pages with original publisher content.
  • Navigation, alert, loading, and empty-result screens should never be treated as ad inventory.

Review Readiness

The site includes an authorized sellers file, visible contact and privacy pages, original guide content, editorial collections, and crawlable library pages. Raw player pages are excluded from the public sitemap path so search and advertising review can focus on content-rich URLs.
