About Getaway Shootout

[[File:Keyart.jpg|360px|link=]]

Getaway Shootout is a local multiplayer racing game. Your goal is to get to the getaway objective while using weapons to outrun other players. The game has a vibrant speedrunning community well-known for pushing the game's limits. We hope you find what you're looking for here!
